T-shirt sizing for Microsoft Fabric projects

This scorecard helps estimate the likely size of a Microsoft Fabric implementation by combining scope, engineering complexity, governance requirements, semantic modeling, reporting needs, and delivery expectations into a single sizing view.

It is intended as an early planning tool for shaping conversations about delivery effort, sequencing, stakeholder alignment, and the level of architecture and platform design that may be required before detailed estimation begins.
